# General-parameter manuscript archive

An earlier general-parameter manuscript is preserved in repository history at commit

`d23fed4692bd04195555d9f176a2ca31e6405918`

under

`projects/erdos-906-sparse-fock/paper/paper_en.tex`.

That version studies the broader family
\[
F_p(z)=\sum_{j\ge1}\frac{z^{\lfloor j^p\rfloor}}{\sqrt{\lfloor j^p\rfloor!}},
\qquad 4/3\le p<2,
\]
and includes mathematical assertions that are not all represented in the current Lean development. It is therefore retained as research material rather than as part of the fixed \(p=3/2\) paper.

The current paper is `../paper/paper_en.tex` and fixes \(p=3/2\). Further general-parameter work is kept in this directory so that the scope of the paper and of the Lean development remains explicit.
