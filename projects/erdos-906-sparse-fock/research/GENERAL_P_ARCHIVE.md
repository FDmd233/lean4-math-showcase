# General-parameter manuscript archive

The pre-split manuscript is preserved exactly in repository history at commit

`d23fed4692bd04195555d9f176a2ca31e6405918`

under

`projects/erdos-906-sparse-fock/paper/paper_en.tex`.

That version studies the broader family

\[
F_p(z)=\sum_{j\ge1}\frac{z^{\lfloor j^p\rfloor}}{\sqrt{\lfloor j^p\rfloor!}},
\qquad 4/3\le p<2,
\]

and includes mathematical assertions that are not all represented in the current Lean development. It is therefore an archived research manuscript, not the canonical website submission.

The canonical paper after the scope split is `../paper/paper_en.tex` and fixes `p = 3/2`. Future research should not edit the canonical paper merely to preserve or extend the general-parameter manuscript; new extensions belong in this directory until they satisfy the promotion rule in `README.md`.
